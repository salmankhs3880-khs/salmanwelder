Google Apps Script Auto-Generator Code

Automatically creates Master_Ledger and Workers tabs (including Worker Staff IDs) in your Google Sheet

  1. Open Google Drive on your Chromebook and create or open your Google Sheet named Salman Welding Workshop Ledger.
  2. In the Google Sheet top menu, click Extensions → Apps Script.
  3. Delete any old code, click Copy Google Apps Script Code above, and paste it into the editor.
  4. Click Deploy → Manage deployments → Edit (pencil icon) → New version → Deploy.
  5. Click Push All Local Records to Sheet in Step 1 so all Worker Staff IDs sync to your sheet!

Step A: Upload index.html to GitHub
